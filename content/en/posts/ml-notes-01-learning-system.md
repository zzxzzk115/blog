The course is Professor Andrew Ng's free machine learning course on NetEase: [Machine Learning](http://mooc.study.163.com/smartSpec/detail/1001319001.htm)

<!--more-->

NetEase runs this course in partnership with Coursera, so the content is the same, but it has Chinese subtitles, which is handy for someone with terrible English like me...

Of course, these notes are what I put together from the videos and the slides (PDF); I'll do my best to translate the key points from the slides...

What the course covers:

1. Neural Networks and Deep Learning
2. Improving Deep Neural Networks: Hyperparameter tuning, Regularization and Optimization
3. Structuring your Machine Learning project
4. Convolutional Neural Networks
5. Natural Language Processing: Building sequence models
