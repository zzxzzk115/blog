## Why Is Deep Learning Taking Off?

Big data, a digitized society, faster computation, and the pace of innovation in neural network algorithms have led to the rise of deep learning.

<!--more-->

![](/img/csdn/79967916-1.png)

Two things are considered key to reaching a high level of performance:

1.Being able to train a big enough neural network

2.A huge amount of labeled data

The process of training a neural network is iterative.

![](/img/csdn/79967916-2.png)

How long it takes to train a neural network affects your productivity. Faster computation helps you iterate and come up with improved new algorithms.
