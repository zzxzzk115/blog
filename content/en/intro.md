Hi, I'm Kexuan Zhang, also known as **Lazy_V**. I'm a PhD student in **Perceptual Graphics at the University of Leeds**. My research interests are virtual reality and high-performance graphics, particularly foveated rendering, stereo reprojection, and image inpainting for disocclusion holes.

I am supervised by Dr. [Rafael Kuffner dos Anjos](https://rafaelkuffner.github.io/), Dr. Markus Billeter, and Prof. Gordon D. Love.

Before starting my PhD, I completed an MSc in **High-Performance Graphics and Games Engineering** at the University of Leeds with Distinction. My MSc covered modelling and rendering, animation and simulation, geometry processing, high-performance graphics, and scientific computation.

Before my master's, I worked as a game server engineer at Blackjack Studio, the Shanghai R&D centre of Zilong Game Limited, mainly with C#, multithreading, networking, and performance optimisation.

## What I'm working on

I build graphics libraries, engines, games, and small tools. My current graphics projects include [VRI](https://github.com/zzxzzk115/VRI), a cross-platform rendering API, and [libvultra](https://github.com/zzxzzk115/libvultra), the graphics framework I am developing on top of it. I am also studying CUDA through CIS5650 independently, with an interest in GPU-driven rendering, large scenes, and global illumination.

This blog is where I keep technical notes, tutorials, and things I learn while experimenting. Posts reflect the tools and versions used when they were written. For current research, projects, and plans, see my [personal homepage](https://zzxzzk115.github.io/).

## Away from research

I collect retro handhelds and enjoy getting games and software running on GameShell, Game Boy, PocketCHIP, Trimui, and whatever else I can get my hands on. I'm also an indie game developer, with games on Steam, itch.io and GitHub, some playable right here (see the [Games Gallery](/game-dev/001-games-gallery)), and an indie musician: I like singing and have written a few tracks, released on [NetEase Cloud Music](https://music.163.com/#/artist?id=33612866). [OmniLyrics](https://github.com/zzxzzk115/OmniLyrics) grew out of wanting a lyric tool that worked the way I wanted. Curiosity and the satisfaction of making something keep me coming back.
